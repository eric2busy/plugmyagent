Agora Video Calling

Battle-tested video, voice, and live chat behind apps like Clubhouse. 10,000 free minutes a month.

What it does

Agora is the behind-the-scenes video and voice engine inside thousands of apps (Clubhouse ran on it). It handles one-on-one calls, group meetings, livestreams with millions of viewers, and in-call chat. New accounts get 10,000 free minutes every month, and if you use them up, your account simply pauses instead of charging you. So there's no surprise bill.

What you can do with it

  • Add crystal-clear video chat to a dating, tutoring, or telehealth app
  • Run a livestream shopping or gaming event for a huge audience
  • Build voice rooms and group calls into a community app

Set it up

1

Get free access

Open the provider's signup page and make a free account.

Sign up for Agora Video Calling ↗
2

Copy your API key

After signing up, look in the dashboard or settings for your API key, then copy it. The provider-specific steps are below.

  1. Sign up and create an Agora project in the console
  2. Enable the video-calling feature and copy your App ID
  3. Generate a temporary access token for testing
  4. Install the SDK for your platform (web, iOS, Android, Flutter, etc.)
  5. Follow the get-started guide to join your first channel and make a call

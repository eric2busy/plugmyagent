DOAJ

The directory of quality open-access journals and articles, free.

What it does

The Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ) lists peer-reviewed open-access journals and articles that meet quality standards. Its free API lets you search journals by subject, check licensing, publisher, article fees, and review policies. Public search needs no key, which makes it easy to verify that a journal is legitimately open access.

What you can do with it

  • Check whether a journal is a real, quality open-access journal before submitting
  • Find open-access articles on a topic for a literature review
  • Look up a journal's article-processing charges and licensing terms

OpenLigaDB

Free community football data focused on German leagues. Scores, tables, and fixtures, no key.

What it does

OpenLigaDB is a community-maintained open database of football match data, strongest on German leagues (Bundesliga down through the 3rd division, going back decades) with other European leagues included. It offers match results, fixtures, standings, and team info through a simple API that needs no key and has no paid tier. It's free as a public resource.

What you can do with it

  • Build a Bundesliga standings and results page for a fan blog
  • Power a match predictor game for your friends with historical fixtures
  • Add next-match fixtures to a supporters' club website

Follow the quick-start steps

Use the provider's instructions below. There's no secret key to manage.

  1. No sign-up. Open the docs at openligadb.de/Api to see all endpoints.
  2. Paste the sample match-data URL into your browser to see real results.
  3. Swap the league shortcut (e.g., bl1 for Bundesliga) and season in the URL.
  4. Use the endpoints directly in your site or app. Live scores depend on community updates.

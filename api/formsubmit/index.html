FormSubmit

Add a working contact form to any website in minutes. No backend, no account, no key.

What it does

FormSubmit is a free service that gives your website's contact or signup form somewhere to send its data. Your email inbox. You just point your form at their address, confirm your email once, and every submission arrives as a nicely formatted email. No signup, no API key, no server code. It even handles file attachments up to 10MB and basic webhooks for free.

What you can do with it

  • Add a contact form to a simple business website with zero backend code
  • Collect newsletter signups or quote requests into your inbox
  • Build lead-capture forms on a landing page in an afternoon

Follow the quick-start steps

Use the provider's instructions below. There's no secret key to manage.

  1. In your HTML form, set the form action to https://formsubmit.co/your@email.com
  2. Submit the form once. FormSubmit emails you a confirmation link
  3. Click the confirmation link to activate your form
  4. All future submissions land in your inbox automatically
  5. Optional: add hidden fields like _subject, _next (redirect page), or _captcha=false to customize

ZenQuotes

Inspirational quotes from famous thinkers, ready to display.

Open in PlugMyAgentOfficial docs

What it does

ZenQuotes offers a curated collection of inspirational quotes you can pull one at a time or in batches. You can filter by author or keyword, get the quote of the day, or even get quotes rendered onto images. Free without a key (with attribution).

What you can do with it

  • A daily inspiration widget on a homepage
  • Motivational quotes in a wellness or journaling app
  • Quote images for social media posts (via their image mode)

Set it up

  1. Read the guide at https://docs.zenquotes.io/zenquotes-documentation/.
  2. Open https://zenquotes.io/api/random for a random quote.
  3. Try https://zenquotes.io/api/today for the quote of the day.
  4. Filter by author, e.g. https://zenquotes.io/api/quotes/author/shakespeare.
  5. Add a small 'quotes by ZenQuotes' credit link on your page.

Example request

https://zenquotes.io/api/random

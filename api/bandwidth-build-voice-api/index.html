About

Bandwidth is a major US voice and messaging network, and their Build plan is a free trial that gets you a real US phone number, a voice application, and 3,000 credits without a credit card. You can make and receive calls with full control over how they're answered, plus set up SIP trunks. The trial limits calls to your verified number, 5 at a time, and 30 minutes each. Plenty to build and test a calling feature.

Setup

  1. Create a free Bandwidth account with no credit card
  2. Verify your phone number to activate the trial
  3. Get your trial US number and set up a voice application
  4. Point the application at your webhook URL
  5. Answer calls and play audio with Bandwidth's voice commands (BXML)
Sign up
Example
Answer a call with BXML on your webhook: <Response><SpeakSentence>Hello from Bandwidth</SpeakSentence></Response>

Use cases

  • Build an automated phone menu (press 1 for sales) for a small business
  • Add click-to-call buttons to a website that ring your team
  • Prototype appointment-reminder calls before buying a plan

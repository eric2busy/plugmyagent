Make (formerly Integromat)

Drag-and-drop automation connecting 3,000+ apps. 1,000 free operations a month.

What it does

Make is the visual automation platform (formerly Integromat) where you draw workflows as flowcharts: trigger → filter → action across 3,000+ apps. The free plan gives you 1,000 operations a month with no credit card, which covers several everyday automations like syncing forms to sheets or auto-posting alerts. Its visual builder makes complex logic approachable without code.

What you can do with it

  • When a form is submitted, add the lead to your CRM and send a welcome email
  • Sync new calendar events into your task manager automatically
  • Watch a spreadsheet for changes and notify your team in Slack

2

Copy your API key

After signing up, look in the dashboard or settings for your API key, then copy it. The provider-specific steps are below.

  1. Sign up free at make.com (no credit card for the free plan)
  2. Click Create a new scenario to open the visual builder
  3. Pick a trigger app (e.g. 'Watch emails' or 'Webhooks') and connect your account
  4. Add action modules after it. Filters and routers handle the logic visually
  5. Save, turn the scenario On, and let it run automatically

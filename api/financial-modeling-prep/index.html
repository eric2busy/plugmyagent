Financial Modeling Prep

Company financials and market data with a no-code-friendly free tier

What it does

Financial Modeling Prep (FMP) offers stock prices, company financial statements, ratios, analyst estimates, and market news through a simple REST API. You can use it to pull a company's income statement for a research sheet, to build a ratio dashboard comparing tech stocks, or to feed end-of-day prices into a spreadsheet with a no-code tool. The free tier is limited to delayed or sandbox data and a modest daily allowance, which is fine for learning and prototypes.

What you can do with it

  • Pull a company's income statement into a research spreadsheet
  • Build a ratio dashboard comparing several stocks
  • Feed end-of-day prices into a no-code tool or sheet

Copy your API key

After signing up, look in the dashboard or settings for your API key, then copy it. The provider-specific steps are below.

  1. Register for a free account on the Financial Modeling Prep site.
  2. Copy your API key from the dashboard.
  3. Open the developer docs and choose an endpoint such as the stock quote.
  4. Try the example URL in your browser with your key in place of YOUR_KEY.
  5. Cache results in your app so the 250-per-day budget lasts.

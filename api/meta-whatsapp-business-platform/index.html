Meta WhatsApp Business Platform

Message customers on WhatsApp with 1,000 free service messages a month per number.

What it does

This is the official way for businesses to send WhatsApp messages at scale. Order updates, appointment reminders, support chats, and marketing templates. As of September 2026, Meta's pricing says service conversations stay free through September 30, 2026; from October 1, 2026 each business number gets 1,000 free service messages per month (you only need billing if you go past that). You'll need a Meta developer account and a verified business.

What you can do with it

  • Send order tracking and delivery updates on WhatsApp
  • Run customer support chats where your customers already are
  • Remind patients or clients of upcoming appointments

Set it up

1

Get free access

Open the provider's signup page and make a free account.

Sign up for Meta WhatsApp Business Platform ↗
2

Copy your API key

After signing up, look in the dashboard or settings for your API key, then copy it. The provider-specific steps are below.

  1. Create a Meta developer account and an app
  2. Add the WhatsApp product and get a test phone number
  3. Send test messages from the dashboard or API
  4. Verify your business and claim your own WhatsApp number
  5. Submit message templates for approval, then go live
3

Tell your agent

Copy the message, paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, or a similar agent. No code needed. Replace PASTE_KEY_HERE with your key first. Your agent will either set it up directly or hand you the exact command to run.

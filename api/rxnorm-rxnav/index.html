RxNorm (RxNav)

Standardize messy drug names into official clinical drug codes, free.

What it does

RxNorm is the National Library of Medicine's service that turns messy drug names into standard, official identifiers. Type something like 'Tylenol' and it finds the exact clinical drug, its strength, dose form, and related products. It is free with no key and is the backbone of most U.S. medication systems.

What you can do with it

  • Match user-typed drug names like 'advil' to the exact official product
  • Link prescriptions to standardized drug codes in a health app
  • Find related brand and generic versions of a medication

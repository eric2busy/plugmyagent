Documentation: newsdata.io · Checked Sep 13, 2026

About

NewsData.io pulls articles, crypto news, and market news from tens of thousands of publishers worldwide. Each request returns up to 10 articles with titles, summaries, images, and links, and you can filter by country, language, or category. The free tier is delayed by 48 hours but explicitly allows commercial use, which is rare among free news APIs.

Setup

  1. Sign up for a free account at newsdata.io.
  2. Copy your API key from the dashboard.
  3. Call the endpoint with ?apikey=YOUR_API_KEY&q=your+topic.
  4. Use the nextPage value in responses to page through more results.
Sign up
Example
https://newsdata.io/api/1/latest?apikey=YOUR_API_KEY&q=technology&language=en

Use cases

  • Build a news widget for a business site without paying
  • Monitor industry or competitor mentions across countries
  • Feed a multilingual news digest to a chatbot or newsletter

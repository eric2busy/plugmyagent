Useless Facts

Random 'did you know?' facts that are useless but fun.

What it does

Useless Facts hands out short, surprising trivia in English or German. One request returns one random fact, or you can grab today's featured fact. Free, no key, and simple to use.

What you can do with it

  • A daily-fact widget on a website
  • Conversation starters in a chat app
  • Loading-screen trivia in games

2

Follow the quick-start steps

Use the provider's instructions below. There's no secret key to manage.

  1. Visit https://uselessfacts.jsph.pl/ to read the short docs.
  2. Open https://uselessfacts.jsph.pl/api/v2/facts/random in your browser.
  3. Add ?language=de for German facts.
  4. Use /api/v2/facts/today for the fact of the day.
  5. No account needed - just start calling it.

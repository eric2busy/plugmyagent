DailyMed

Official FDA drug labels: uses, warnings, dosages, and ingredients, free.

What it does

DailyMed is the U.S. National Library of Medicine's service that publishes official FDA drug labeling. Its free API returns the same information you find on medication inserts: approved uses, warnings, dosage instructions, side effects, ingredients, and packaging. Everything is free with no key and comes straight from the official drug label.

What you can do with it

  • Look up the official side effects and warnings for any prescription drug
  • Build a medication reference tool with trusted FDA label data
  • Show drug packaging images and ingredient lists in a pharmacy helper app

Deezer API

A key-free REST API for searching tracks, albums, artists, and playlists from the Deezer music catalog.

What it does

Deezer is a major music streaming service, and its public API is one of the easiest music APIs around: no signup, no key, just URLs. You can search millions of tracks, albums, and artists, pull cover art, track lengths, and 30-second previews. It's ideal when you want music data with zero setup friction.

What you can do with it

  • A restaurant website adds a 'listen to a sample' player for its background playlist
  • A music teacher's site auto-fills song lengths and album art for lesson plans
  • A fan forum embeds album covers and previews next to song mentions

Follow the quick-start steps

Use the provider's instructions below. There's no secret key to manage.

  1. No signup needed; open developers.deezer.com/api to browse endpoints
  2. Try a search right in your browser: api.deezer.com/search?q=daft punk
  3. Look through the JSON for track names, previews, and cover art URLs
  4. Paste the endpoint into your site or app and use the data directly

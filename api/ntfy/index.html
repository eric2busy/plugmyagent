ntfy

Push notifications to your phone from any script with one simple line. No signup, no cost, no key.

What it does

ntfy (pronounced 'notify') is a beautifully simple free service: you pick a topic name, subscribe to it in the free ntfy app, and anything sent to that topic address pops up on your phone like a text message. Sending is literally one command. It's fully open source and can also be self-hosted. No account, no API key, no credit card. Just a topic name.

What you can do with it

  • Get a phone alert when a long backup, download, or script finishes
  • Receive uptime-monitor alerts on your phone without a paid service
  • Have your smart-home or Raspberry Pi text your phone with sensor events

Follow the quick-start steps

Use the provider's instructions below. There's no secret key to manage.

  1. Install the free ntfy app on your phone from your app store
  2. Open the app and tap to subscribe to a topic. Make up a random, hard-to-guess name like 'erics-server-alerts-9x7'
  3. That's the setup. Now anyone who knows the topic can send to it with: curl -d "message here" ntfy.sh/your-topic-name
  4. Send a test and watch the notification pop up on your phone
  5. Wire it into your scripts, website, or automation tool as the alert channel

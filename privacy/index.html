Privacy Policy

The short version: no accounts, no sign-ups, no selling data. This page explains the few things the site does touch.

Stored on your device

Your theme choice and your My Stack list are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

Hosting and visit counts

The site is hosted on Vercel. As with any web host, standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) is processed to serve pages and keep the service secure. We look at aggregate visit counts, meaning which pages get read, not who read them. This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

The tools run locally

The utilities on the Tools page, including the API Key Checker, run entirely in your browser. Anything you paste into them is never transmitted or stored.

Links to other sites

The catalog links out to APIs, code repositories, and documentation run by other people and companies. Their privacy policies apply once you leave this site. Some guides include a referral code for Muse; it is labeled as one wherever it appears, and using it is always optional.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its date. Questions: reach us through @hiddeninwest on X or at hiddeninwest.com.
